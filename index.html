// ABOUT

Ownership, on-device.

For the past 8+ years I've been building production Swift applications — the last several focused almost entirely on on-device AI and computer vision. At PackageX, I designed and built the iOS component of the company's Vision SDK: a computer-vision and OCR engine that now runs on-device across 40+ countries, cutting document processing time from 7+ seconds to roughly 200ms while lifting extraction accuracy to ~95%. I own that platform's iOS side end-to-end, migrated every client app onto it, and mentor the engineers building alongside me.

Before that, at SixLogics, I led a team, trained interns, and personally shipped 27+ iOS apps — including two years as the sole developer behind Betway Scores, where I built an entire live-scoring, news, and notifications system from scratch.

I work across MVVM, VIPER, and Clean Architecture, and I care as much about the engineers around me as I do about the code — mentoring, code review, and technical leadership are as much a part of the job as Swift itself.
